HOW IT WORKS · Two pools of six, split by playoff qualification. Within each pool, balls are awarded by finish: the best finisher in the pool gets 6, down to 1 for the worst. The non-playoff pool draws for picks 1–6; the playoff pool draws for picks 7–12. Over-cap teams (per the Aug 31 Tax Day snapshot) take a cap penalty — their lottery weight is reduced by (tax owed ÷ $10M) × 5%, down to a floor of 25% retained. The reduced weight redistributes to under-cap teams in the same pool, proportional to cap room. The draw itself is run live off-app and results are logged here after — odds shown are final and locked before the draw.
