# 40s and Blunts Weekly Rolling Coverage

### Week 24 · Both #1 seeds. Same week. Gone.

---

Stone Jack Ballers won the overall #1 seed by a single game in the final week
of the regular season. Chief Noc-A-Homa was the runner-up in that race and
the #2 seed everywhere else. Between them they'd been the top two teams in
this league for six months. This week, both of them lost.

Chicago beat Stone Jack Ballers. Loosh beat Chief. The Championship comes
down to a 5-seed and a 3-seed — nobody's top seed, nobody's Regular Season
Champion, just the two teams that showed up and played the better week when
it counted.

Worth sitting with what each of them actually is. Chicago went 1-9 in Week
20 — the worst single week this newsletter tracked all season — and is now
one series from a title. This franchise has won it before, twice, as Dream
of the 1890s in 2012 and The Fight'n Lincolns in 2016. Loosh has nine
championships already, the most in league history by a wide margin. One more
win and it's ten.

---

**CHAMPIONSHIP SEMIFINALS**

**L-A-Loosh 5, Chief Noc-A-Homa 4, one tie.** Loosh took runs, home runs, RBI,
steals, and OPS. Chief answered with strikeouts, ERA, WHIP, and quality
starts. Saves-plus-holds tied. Nine championships in franchise history, and
the winningest team in this league's twenty-plus years just knocked out the
Regular Season Champion's closest rival in a one-category game.

**The Chicago Way 5, Stone Jack Ballers 3, two ties.** Chicago took home
runs, RBI, OPS, strikeouts, and WHIP. Stone Jack Ballers took runs, ERA, and
saves-plus-holds. Steals and quality starts both tied. The Regular Season
Champion is out of the Championship bracket in the very next round.

---

**5TH PLACE**

**Bad MoFo's 10, Section 34 0.** A complete reversal of last week, and about
as clean a sweep as this format allows. Bad MoFo's takes 5th outright.
Section 34 finishes 6th.

**11TH PLACE**

**Old Fashioned 7, Thurgood Jenkins 3.** Thurgood finishes the season 12th
out of 12 — dead last in the entire league.

---

**TOILET BOWL SEMIFINALS**

**Bip Roberts 10, MayTheSchwarBeWithU 0.** The second 10-0 sweep of the
weekend, and worth remembering who delivered the first one back in Week 20 —
Stone Jack Ballers, 10-0 over Bip Roberts, in that week's Matchup of the
Week. Bip just handed out the exact result it once absorbed. As the reigning
league champion, missing the real bracket by 1.5 games was already a
letdown — advancing to the 7th Place Game is Bip clawing its way back to the
best finish still available to it.

**Grand Theft Votto 6, Bell Biv Devo 4.** Votto peaked at 8-2 and 9-1 across
back-to-back weeks in August, then lost the one game that would have made
the playoff field — to Loosh, of all teams, in the final week of the regular
season. Missed the bracket by 4.5 games and has fought back to the same
7th Place Game as Bip. One win from turning a heartbreak into the best
possible ending for a season that fell just short.
Bell Biv Devo beat Chief in Week 20 and Loosh in Week 21 — two scalps off two
Championship teams — and it finally ran out of magic against Votto, at the
one point in the schedule where losing actually ended something.
