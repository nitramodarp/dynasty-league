# 40s and Blunts Weekly Rolling Coverage

### Week 20 · The one where both leaders got run over

---

**MATCHUP OF THE WEEK**

Yahoo picked Bad MoFo's vs. Section 34, and the underdog left no doubt. Section
34 won seven categories outright — runs, home runs, RBI, OPS, strikeouts, ERA,
WHIP — and tied saves-plus-holds. Bad MoFo's kept stolen bases and quality
starts and lost the rest, 7–2–1. Section 34 climbs into the final playoff spot.
Bad MoFo's drops to fifth.

---

**LAST WEEK'S RESULTS**

**Bell Biv Devo 7, Chief Noc-A-Homa 3.** Ninth place took the league leader apart on offense and both ratios, 32 runs to 23, an
.846 team OPS, saves-plus-holds 7–3. Chief kept stolen bases, strikeouts, and
quality starts. The lead grew anyway, because second place had a worse week.

**MayTheSchwarBeWithU 8, SJ Ballers 2.** Second place posted a 4.95 ERA and lost
eight categories to a team twenty-one games under .500.

**Grand Theft Votto 9, The Chicago Way 1.** Votto lost only quality starts —

**L-A-Loosh 7, Old Fashioned 1.** This was flagged last week as the sixth-seed
decider. It isn't the sixth seed anymore — Loosh is fourth outright now.

**Bip Roberts 8, Thurgood Jenkins 2.** Thurgood managed OPS and saves-plus-holds;
Bip had everything else. Third place, and gaining.

**QUESTIONABLE ACTIVITY**

**SJ Ballers ran five add/drop cycles in forty-one minutes Saturday morning,**
4:22 to 5:03 a.m. Nolan Arenado — an actual All-Star third baseman — was on the
roster for twenty-three minutes before getting waived for Chandler Simpson.
Call it a bad Tweezer: long, aimless, and it just kept going.

**MayTheSchwarBeWithU added Jordan Romano, dropped him a minute later for Edward
Cabrera, then re-added Romano three minutes after that** — the same shape as the
Andrew Painter sequence two weeks ago. This is a house style now, not an
accident.

**ON THE TRADE BLOCK**

The block worked. Chief posted "HR, OPS Wanted" Tuesday; by Wednesday morning,
Jackson Holliday and Max Clark were headed to Grand Theft Votto for Bryson
Stott. One trade, one day — and it sent a ripple through both rosters.

The rest is quiet. L-A-Loosh still has Jeremy Peña listed, unchanged since last
Wednesday. Nine teams have nothing posted.

**PLAYOFF PICTURE**

The sixth seed changed hands again. Section 34 is in; Chicago is out, four spots
removed in one week. Grand Theft Votto is the new closest chaser, 1.5 games
behind Section 34 and playing better than anyone in the league over the past two
weeks. Two weeks to play.

---

**PLAYOFF STRUCTURE**

Six teams. Weeks 23, 24, and 25, ending Sunday, September 20. Seeding is by
overall standings — the Colt 45 and Olde English split decides nothing once the
bracket sets. The top two seeds get a bye into the semifinal round; seeds three
through six open the bracket in Week 23. The field reseeds after each round.

Money: $240 in the pool. Champion takes $132. Runner-up takes $48. Each
division winner takes $18. Best regular-season record takes $12. Toilet Bowl
loser takes $12. Every game from here forward is worth something to somebody.

**WEEK 21 PREVIEW**

Last week before the bracket sets. Every game below has a number attached to it.

Yahoo's Matchup of the Week: **Bip Roberts (3rd) vs. SJ Ballers (2nd)** — 7.5
games apart, and the closest thing to a direct bye race on the board. SJ still
has the safer cushion, but a Bip win pulls the second bye spot from
comfortable to contested with one week left to settle it.

- **Chief Noc-A-Homa vs. Grand Theft Votto** — the coldest week in the league
against the hottest. Chief's bye isn't in danger from this game, but Votto is
1.5 out of the field and needs statement wins over good teams to hold off
Section 34's lead on the tiebreaker. Beat the leader here and the case writes
itself.

- **Bad MoFo's vs. Thurgood Jenkins** — Bad MoFo's is already 1.5 back of the
fourth and fifth seeds after last week's loss and cannot afford a second one.
Thurgood is out of the race and has nothing to lose playing spoiler.

- **Bell Biv Devo vs. L-A-Loosh** — Loosh just climbed into a bye-adjacent spot
and draws the team that beat the league's number one overall club four
categories to none last week. Bell Biv Devo is mathematically buried and
temperamentally dangerous.

- **MayTheSchwarBeWithU vs. Section 34** — Section 34 is holding the sixth seed
by 1.5 games over Grand Theft Votto and needs a result here to keep it. MaySchwar
is eliminated and gets to decide, essentially for free, whether that margin
grows or disappears.

- **Old Fashioned vs. The Chicago Way** — Chicago is twenty games back with two
weeks left, which is not mathematically dead but is close enough that this one
functions as a must-win in practice. Old Fashioned has played the same way all
season regardless of the stakes, which in this case works entirely in
Chicago's favor.

Trade deadline: Thursday, August 20. Three days.
