# 40s and Blunts Weekly Rolling Coverage
### Week 18 · The one decided by a rain delay in Baltimore

---

**MATCHUP OF THE WEEK**

Yahoo designated **Chief Noc-A-Homa vs. SJ Ballers** the matchup of the week back
on Monday, which was a rare instance of the algorithm calling its shot. First
place against second place, and it came down to two strikeouts.

Chief won 5–4. Read the counting stats and you would never guess it. SJ Ballers
scored 38 runs to 28, hit 10 home runs to 8, drove in 33 to 25, and played 106
games to Chief's 87 — nineteen more player-games, and a win in every offensive
category that involves counting things.

He lost anyway, on the two categories where volume does not help you. Chief's
staff put up a 2.22 ERA and a 0.99 WHIP against 4.31 and 1.46. OPS went to Chief
by .704 to .698 — six thousandths, which is the kind of margin that makes a
person want to go back and look at a specific at-bat from Tuesday.

And then strikeouts, 56 to 54. Zack Wheeler was pitching Sunday in Baltimore
when the rain came. He got two innings and three strikeouts before they took him
off the field. A normal Wheeler start clears two punchouts before the third
inning is over, and the category flips, and so does the week.

Jacob Misiorowski went ten strikeouts, 2.57, 0.43 for Chief on the other side.
Somewhere in there is a lesson about roster construction, and somewhere else in
there is a weather radar over Camden Yards.

**LAST WEEK'S RESULTS**

**Chief Noc-A-Homa def. SJ Ballers, 5–4.** See above. Stolen bases finished tied
at four apiece, which is the only reason this was not 5–5 and the only reason
anyone gets to feel good about it.

**L-A-Loosh def. Bad MoFo's, 7–2.** The bear is awake and has been for a month
now. Loosh took runs 38–21, home runs 12–2, RBI, steals, OPS .776 to .586, ERA,
and WHIP. Bad MoFo's answered with strikeouts and saves-plus-holds. Quality
starts finished tied at three. Twelve home runs to two is not a matchup result,
it is a weather report, and Bad MoFo's spent the week in it.

**Grand Theft Votto def. Bip Roberts, 8–2.** Votto scored 39 runs to 19 and
posted a .972 OPS against .671. They took runs, homers, RBI, steals, OPS,
strikeouts, quality starts, and saves. Bip got ERA and WHIP and a long ride home.
This one has consequences; see the playoff picture.

**Section 34 def. Old Fashioned, 7–2.** Section 34 ran a 2.13 ERA and a 0.99 WHIP
and took runs, RBI, steals, OPS, strikeouts, and both ratios. Old Fashioned held
home runs 11–9 and saves-plus-holds 4–3. Quality starts tied. Section 34 needed
this one more than they let on.

**MayTheSchwarBeWithU def. Bell Biv Devo, 8–2.** Eight categories, none of them
especially close: runs, homers, steals, OPS, strikeouts, ERA, WHIP, and
saves-plus-holds 8–2. Bell Biv Devo kept RBI 21–19 and quality starts 5–4. For a
team sitting tenth, MayTheSchwar has been playing like a problem for three weeks.

**The Chicago Way def. Thurgood Jenkins, 5–4.** The second one-run week of the
day. Chicago took home runs, RBI, OPS, ERA, and quality starts. Thurgood took
runs 26–25, steals, strikeouts 55–33, and WHIP. Saves-plus-holds tied at six.
Thurgood struck out twenty-two more batters than his opponent and lost the week,
which is a sentence that should be read slowly.

**QUESTIONABLE ACTIVITY**

**SJ Ballers conducted a seven-minute transaction event on Sunday evening.** At
7:05 p.m. he dropped AJ Smith-Shawver. At 7:06 he added Jack Dreyer and dropped
Jacob deGrom. At 7:08 he added Casey Mize and dropped Jack Dreyer. At 7:12 he
added Andruw Monasterio and dropped Casey Mize. Dreyer was a member of this
organization for two minutes. Mize lasted four. Monasterio went into the lineup
in time for the Sunday night game, scored a run, and is presumably still there.

This was also the night he lost by two strikeouts. The league offers no comment
and does not need to.

**Bip Roberts added Brandon Nimmo at 3:30 in the morning on Monday and dropped
him Friday afternoon.** He also added Emilio Pagán at 3:24 a.m., six minutes
earlier. Whatever was happening at 3:24 in the morning on July 27th, it involved
a roster.

**L-A-Loosh ran a genuine carousel.** Added Landen Roupp at 7:42 a.m. Monday,
dropped him for Wilyer Abreu at 2:20 p.m. the same day, then dropped Abreu the
next afternoon for Heriberto Hernández. Also added Devin Williams, Justin
Slaten, and — on Saturday — Nico Hoerner, whom Bip had dropped on Monday. Seventy-one
moves on the season and a 7–2–1 week to go with them. The method is not
obvious but the results are.

**Thurgood Jenkins added Michael McGreevy on Tuesday morning and dropped him
Wednesday morning,** which is a streamer executed to specification. He also
dropped Gage Jump, whom Bell Biv Devo picked up on Saturday.

**Bell Biv Devo made five moves and lost 8–2.** Merrill Kelly, Gage Jump, Andre
Pallante, Luis Rengifo in and then back out again two days later. The activity
was there. The results were elsewhere.

**Chief Noc-A-Homa added Ty France on Monday and dropped him Friday**, in between
adding Erik Miller and Dylan Crews on Wednesday evening. Also a partial
correction to last week: the commissioner is not above the Ty France cycle.

**Old Fashioned and Grand Theft Votto made no moves at all.** Grand Theft Votto
won 8–2. Old Fashioned lost 7–2 and has made one roster move all season — the
league has made 568. Both facts are offered without editorial guidance.

**PLAYOFF PICTURE**

Bip Roberts entered Week 18 in fourth place. He is now seventh, and out.

A 2–8 loss to Grand Theft Votto did it, and the timing was unfortunate: Section
34 went 7–2–1 in the same week and passed him for the sixth and final spot,
82-79-19 to 86-85-9. Bip has four more wins than Section 34 and is watching from
outside, which is what nineteen ties will do for a team.

The field as it stands: Chief, SJ Ballers, Bad MoFo's, L-A-Loosh, The Chicago
Way, Section 34. Bip is one spot out with four weeks to play. Bell Biv Devo,
Grand Theft Votto, and MayTheSchwar are all within striking distance of nothing
in particular but have not been eliminated, and MayTheSchwar has won eight
categories in a week twice this month.

At the top, Chief leads SJ Ballers by five for the first bye. Bad MoFo's sit
12.5 back of first and seven and a half back of second, which makes the second
bye a two-team conversation unless something breaks.
