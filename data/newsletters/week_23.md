# 40s and Blunts Weekly Rolling Coverage

### Week 23 · The bracket separates the contenders from the field

---

The Chicago Way just delivered the most lopsided result of the playoffs —
nine categories to one — and takes the 5-seed into a semifinal against Stone
Jack Ballers, still the #1 overall seed. Loosh did exactly what this
newsletter said it would three weeks ago: the Power Rankings called it the
second-hottest team left in the bracket and flagged Bad MoFo's as a bad draw
for it. Loosh went out and beat Bad MoFo's, 6–3–1, and now gets Chief in the
semifinal. Worth knowing what Loosh actually is heading into that game — nine
championships, most in league history by a wide margin, more than triple the
next-closest franchise. A Chief team that was the coldest in the Championship
bracket heading into its bye just drew the winningest team this league has
ever had.

Chief, Stone Jack Ballers, Bip Roberts, and Grand Theft Votto had byes this
week. Nothing happened to them, and nothing was supposed to. Everyone else
plays on regardless of Sunday's result — Section 34 and Bad MoFo's meet again
for 5th place, Thurgood and Old Fashioned for 11th — but this week's real
business was Chicago and Loosh punching their tickets to join Chief and Stone
Jack Ballers as the four teams still alive for the actual thing.

---

**CHAMPIONSHIP QUARTERFINALS**

**The Chicago Way 9, Section 34 1.** Section 34 kept stolen bases. That's the
whole box score. Chicago swept runs, home runs, RBI, OPS, strikeouts, ERA,
WHIP, quality starts, and saves-plus-holds — the most one-sided result this
newsletter has covered all season, in the one week it actually eliminated
somebody. Section 34, which beat Chicago in every single meeting during the
regular season, goes to the 5th-place game against Bad MoFo's.

**L-A-Loosh 6, Bad MoFo's 3, one tie.** Loosh took runs, home runs, RBI, OPS,
ERA, and saves-plus-holds. Bad MoFo's answered with strikeouts, WHIP, and
quality starts. Stolen bases tied. This tracked exactly with where both teams
stood coming in — Loosh was the third-hottest team in the bracket over the
final three weeks of the season, Bad MoFo's the second-coldest. The numbers
said this was coming. Bad MoFo's now plays Section 34 for 5th.

---

**TOILET BOWL QUARTERFINALS**

MayTheSchwarBeWithU beat Thurgood Jenkins, 6–4. Bell Biv Devo beat Old
Fashioned, 6–3–1. Thurgood and Old Fashioned now play each other for 11th
place, which is either the least consequential game left on the schedule or
the only one that's honest about what it is.

through the #1 overall seed to do anything with it. Loosh, playing the best
baseball of anyone left in the bracket over the season's final stretch,

Two weeks to the Championship. Everyone else is still just as alive as they
were last week — for something.
