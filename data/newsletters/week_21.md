# 40s and Blunts Weekly Rolling Coverage

### Week 21 · The last week that didn't matter, setting up the one that does

---

Chief Noc-A-Homa spent nineteen weeks as the best team in this league. It is no
longer the best team in this league. Stone Jack Ballers swept the Matchup of
the Week 10–0, and the ten-category punch was enough to pass Chief for the
overall #1 seed with one week left to play. Both teams are locked into the
playoffs regardless. What they're playing for now is which name goes on top of
the bracket, and $12 that comes with it.

---

**MATCHUP OF THE WEEK**

**Stone Jack Ballers 10, Bip Roberts 0.** Every category. Runs, homers, RBI,
steals, OPS, strikeouts, ERA, WHIP, quality starts, saves-plus-holds — all ten,
none of them close. Bip came in third place and leaves eighth, the largest
single-week fall this newsletter has tracked all season. Stone Jack Ballers
leaves with the overall #1 seed and a week to defend it.

---

**LAST WEEK'S RESULTS**

**Grand Theft Votto 6, Chief Noc-A-Homa 3.** Votto took runs, homers, RBI,
steals, strikeouts, and OPS; Chief kept both ratios and saves-plus-holds, with
quality starts tied. Votto made two roster moves Sunday morning, before the
final slate — not a victory lap, a closing argument. It worked.

**The Chicago Way 8, Old Fashioned 1.** Chicago took eight straight and lost
only saves-plus-holds, with quality starts tied. Two weeks after a 1-9 week
nearly buried this team, it's playing for its playoff life instead.

**Bell Biv Devo 6, L-A-Loosh 4.** Bell Biv Devo took the offense outright —
runs, homers, RBI, steals, OPS, strikeouts — and lost the rest. This is the
second top-four team Bell Biv Devo has beaten in three weeks, from ninth place.

**Section 34 6, MayTheSchwarBeWithU 4.** Section 34 needed this to hold its
playoff spot and got it clean — runs, OPS, strikeouts, WHIP, quality starts,
saves-plus-holds.

**Bad MoFo's 7, Thurgood Jenkins 3.** Bad MoFo's took seven categories and a
0.70 team WHIP. Thurgood's 61 strikeouts were the best number in this box
score and worth exactly one category.

**REGULAR SEASON CHAMPION — $12**

Stone Jack Ballers .598. Chief Noc-A-Homa .590. One and a half games, one week
left, and it's the only thing separating these two teams now — both are
already locked into the playoffs. Chief closes at home against Chicago.
Stone Jack Ballers closes against Section 34. Whoever wins their own game more
convincingly probably keeps the top line on the bracket.

Both division titles are already decided under the league's actual rule — best

record. Chief has Colt 45 handled at .590 against a next-closest of .524.
Stone Jack Ballers has Olde English at .598 against a next-closest of .531.
Eighteen dollars each, spoken for.

**THE BUBBLE — TWO SPOTS, THREE TEAMS, ONE WEEK**

Chicago (5th) and Section 34 (6th) are both holding what they have. Chicago's
1.5 games clear of the field below it. Section 34 is a half-game behind
Chicago and a full game clear of the next chaser. That chaser is Grand Theft
Votto — seventh place, 18 back, playing the best baseball of anyone in this
league across the last three weeks, and running out of season to prove it.
Bip Roberts sits eighth, 19.5 back and three behind Chicago — alive, barely,
after Sunday's sweep cost it the ground it would have needed.

None of these three teams play each other in the final week. Chicago plays the
team fighting to reclaim the #1 spot. Votto plays a top-four team. Section 34 plays
the team fighting Chief for the top of the bracket. Nobody controls their own
fate directly — all three just have to win, and hope the scoreboard breaks
their way.
